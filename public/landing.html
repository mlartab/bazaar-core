How it works

  1. DiscoverBrowse approved listings by category.
  2. NegotiateCounter offers until both sides accept.
  3. EscrowBuyer funds lock the moment terms agree.
  4. Verify & SettleMeta Vision verdict releases payment.

Escrow

Funds lock on agreement and move only on verified delivery. Every mutation rechecks the invariant, with automatic rollback.

AI Verification

Deliveries pass a fast synchronous check, then Meta Vision review. Inconclusive verdicts freeze escrow.

Slashing

Rejected deliveries refund the buyer. Every transition is an event — misbehavior leaves a trail, not a loss.
